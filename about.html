Who We Are

Riverbend Yoga Studio is a welcoming space where people can focus on their health and well-being. We believe yoga should be available to everyone, no matter their age or experience level.

Meet Our Instructors

Sarah Johnson

Sarah enjoys helping students improve their flexibility and feel more confident through yoga. She teaches beginner and gentle yoga classes.

Michael Lee

Michael focuses on movement, breathing, and balance. He teaches Vinyasa and restorative yoga classes.

Our Studio Values

  • Respect and kindness toward every student.
  • A comfortable space for beginners and experienced students.
  • Supporting physical and mental well-being.
  • Encouraging students to move at their own pace.

Accessibility and Accommodations

We want everyone to feel welcome. Students can ask instructors about pose modifications and other accommodations before class.

Studio Policies

Please arrive a few minutes before class begins. Wear comfortable clothing and bring a water bottle. Be respectful of other students and follow instructor guidance.
